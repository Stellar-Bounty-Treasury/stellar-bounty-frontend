# Stellar Community Fund (SCF) Grant Proposal

- **Project Name**: Stellar Bounty Treasury
- **Track**: Infrastructure & Developer Tooling / Public Goods
- **Funding Requested**: $45,000 (USDC / XLM)

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Ecosystem grants and developer bounties across web3 suffer from severe capital inefficiency, slow verification turnaround, and arbitrary unilateral dispute rejections. Grant administrators struggle to track milestone deliverables, while contributors face non-payment risks.

## 2. Solution: Trustless Milestone Treasury on Soroban
Stellar Bounty Treasury delivers:
- Atomic milestone escrow locked in Soroban smart contracts.
- Automated basis point distribution for team bounties.
- Decentralized dispute arbitration preventing fund capture.
- Plug-and-play SEP-10 authentication and SEP-0001 discovery.

## 3. Roadmap & Milestones
- **Milestone 1**: Mainnet smart contract deployment and formal audit (Month 1).
- **Milestone 2**: Multi-token liquidity router integration (Month 2).
- **Milestone 3**: DAO governance voting sidecar and ecosystem onboarding (Month 3).
