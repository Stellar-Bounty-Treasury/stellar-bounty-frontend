# Stellar Bounty Treasury: 10-Slide Pitch Deck

### Slide 1: Title
**Stellar Bounty Treasury**: Trustless Milestone Escrow & Grant Distribution on Soroban.

### Slide 2: The Problem
Web3 grant programs lose up to 35% of committed capital to abandoned deliverables and manual dispute friction.

### Slide 3: The Solution
Soroban-powered smart contract escrow where funds release conditionally upon cryptographic milestone approval.

### Slide 4: Technology Stack
Soroban Rust WASM, Stellar Asset Contracts (SAC), Node 25 native SQLite, and React 18 multi-wallet client.

### Slide 5: Unique Stellar Advantages
Sub-cent transaction fees ($0.00001), 5-second ledger finality, and native Stellar Asset Contracts.

### Slide 6: Product Architecture
Full-stack diagrams detailing contracts, backend indexer, RPC simulation proxy, and client SDK.

### Slide 7: Business Model & Sustainability
Self-sustaining protocol fee capped at 500 basis points (5.0%) routed directly to community treasury.

### Slide 8: Traction & Verification
300 high-impact improvements, 39 formal invariant & unit tests, and full testnet verification.

### Slide 9: Roadmap
Mainnet launch, cross-chain anchor bridging (SEP-24), and DAO governance module.
