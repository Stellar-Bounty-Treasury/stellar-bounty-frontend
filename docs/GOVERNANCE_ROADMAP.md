# Governance & Decentralization Roadmap

## Phase 1: Multisig Administration (Current)
- Creator and platform administrator roles.
- Designated arbitrator address for dispute resolution.

## Phase 2: DAO Evaluator Council
- Milestone approvals transitioned to DAO tokenholders.
- Quadratic voting mechanism on deliverable reviews.

## Phase 3: Fully Autonomous Dispute Court
- Staked juror arbitration inspired by Kleros / Aragon Court on Soroban.
